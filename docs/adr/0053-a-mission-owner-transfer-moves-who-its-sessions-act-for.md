# 53. A mission owner transfer moves who its sessions act for

Status: accepted, 2026-11-15. Builds on ADR 0032 (a session is a machine principal recorded as acting for the mission owner), ADR 0005 and 0011 (attention items are derived and notify once under the per-mission throttle) and ADR 0047 (session credentials end with their fence).

A mission's owner could not change: a person who left a team, went on leave or handed a mission over kept receiving its attention items and kept being the person its sessions acted for.

## Decision
1. **One person's command.** `mission.owner.transfer` (`missionId`, `newOwnerId`, optional `expectedVersion`) hands an open mission to another person. Only the current owner or a workspace administrator (`workspace:admin`) may run it; it is classified refused for sessions (human-only), and a closed mission is not handed over.
2. **The new owner must be able to stand behind the mission.** The autonomy policy lists plan operations (task and team changes, accepting decisions), which a coordinator session proposes with `mission:write` alone; no operation needs more than the `agent-session` scopes, so the check is the same whatever the policy. They must be an active human of the organization with an active workspace membership, holding every scope of the `agent-session` role (workspace read and mission work) and, while the mission starts its own tasks (auto-start), workflow dispatch (the dispatch of coordinator wakes carries its own explicit scopes). A session never holds more than the person it acts for, so a new owner who could not do what the mission's sessions do is refused with the missing scopes named.
3. **Audited.** The change is one transaction on the mission (owner and version) and one audit event `mission.ownerTransferred` carrying the acting person, the old owner, the new owner and a sentence naming both. The mission's own event log records `mission.ownerChanged`, shown in the new owner's while-you-were-away summary.
4. **Attention and notifications follow, derived.** Attention items are derived for the workspace and Needs you is not per person, so nothing is moved: notification recipients read the mission's owner when the pass runs. The new owner is told of items still open (they have never been told them) under their own per-mission window and inbox; the previous owner is not told again of new items. No item is copied or re-created.
5. **Session principals.** The principals of the mission's team members (`agent:<mission>:<member>`) are re-pointed to the new owner in the same step. A running attempt keeps its credential, its fence and its lease untouched (a transfer revokes nothing and moves no fence), but because a credential resolves its principal's scopes from the person it acts for at each request, its next command is already authorized and attributed as acting for the new owner. Any later attempt binds to the mission's owner as read at its start. If the new owner later loses the workspace, the sessions hold nothing, as before, until an administrator hands the mission on.

## Limits
- The dialog's people come from `missionOwnerCandidates.list` (needs `mission:write`), because `members.list` needs `members:read`, which an operator who owns a mission lacks.
- A transfer is not an approval or a permission grant: the new owner needs the workspace role first.
- No hand-over notice is sent to the new owner beyond the items they are now told about and the while-you-were-away entry.
