# UI polish pass, track E (2026-11-11)

Dated notes, not a description of the present. Screens were captured from a local instance seeded by `tests/support/browserDemo.ts` (plus a fresh first-run workspace) at 1440 and 768 light and 390 and 1440 dark, before and after the change. Captures are kept outside the repo (`~/.cache/shots-before`, `~/.cache/shots-after`); the throwaway capture specs were not committed.

## Before (2026-11-11, base `12dd18a`)
- Full browser suite at 1440-light, 390-dark, 768-light: 186 passed, 21 failed. Real failures, not load: four admin/operational/log specs still expected a `Missions` heading after creating a workspace (landing is now `Today`); the shared result page rendered a white canvas under dark-theme ink (contrast 1.6 to 2.3, axe serious); `vitals` initial JS 274 KB against a 250 KB budget; visual baselines stale or missing for 768.
- Raw ids visible: `Asked by <principal id>` on Today and Approvals, run and work-item ids in the mission Logs tab, `session-xxxx` titles on Live, run job ids in the Live rail.
- First-run: the plan's `Confirm this plan` sat below the steps, far below the fold on a six-step plan; 390 px had 26 px tab buttons, 28 px theme toggle, 25 px breadcrumb links.
- Schedules list used one grid column, leaving the card at about 400 px on a 1440 screen; copy said "from Governor" in several user-visible strings.

## Changes
- Design language in `apps/web/src/index.css` (polish layer): one focus ring (2 px accent, 2 px offset) for every interactive element in both themes; press feedback on buttons; page content eases in once; reduced motion stays honoured by the existing global rule; themed `body` canvas so pages outside the shell (shared result, sign-in, wallboard) follow dark mode.
- Plan decision bar (`PlanCanvas`): Confirm, Edit steps and Reject now live in one sticky bar. Desktop: top of the plan area, follows the reader. Phone: bottom, safe-area aware, Confirm full width over Edit and Reject. One primary action.
- Friendly names: `app.personName(id)` gives "you", the member's display name or "a teammate". Used on Today, Approvals list, detail, history and review dialog. Live titles fall back to "<agent> session", rail runs to "Run"; the mission Logs tab names each run by its task.
- Microcopy: user-visible "Governor" became "Brain"; Schedules and Today descriptions shortened; Schedules empty state tells you what to do next; session filter placeholder no longer says "id".
- Touch targets at 639 px and below: 44 px minimum for buttons, selects, tabs, toggle items, header buttons, breadcrumb links, text inputs.
- Schedules list is full width.
- Tests: `Today` heading in four specs, `Brain could not complete this read` in the a11y spec.

## After (2026-11-11)
- Full browser suite at 1440-light, 390-dark, 768-light after the first round: 199 passed, 8 failed (visual baselines x3 by design, vitals JS budget x3, 768 shell INP 224 ms against 200 under load, primitives 390 expecting 28 px buttons). Then fixed: primitives now expects 44 px on phones; baselines regenerated; `LocalModelSetup` split out of the Today chunk.
- Still failing: `vitals.spec.ts` Today initial JS 264 KB against the 250 KB budget (was 274 KB). Biggest Today loads: Select 27 KB, TodayPage 19 KB, form 15 KB, StartBox 12 KB. A real budget decision, not a polish one. The 768 INP failure is timing sensitive and was not reproduced alone.
- Screens examined: every route listed in the capture (Today, Missions, New mission, mission Live, Plan, Outputs, Team, Brief, Logs, closed mission, Library, Plays, Schedules, Live, Logs, Outputs, Knowledge, review inbox, Approvals and review, Runners, Quality, Setup, Settings, Integrations, Studio, TV, ticket, incident, nine Administration tabs) at 1440-light, 1440-dark, 768-light and 390-dark, plus first-run Today, Today with a draft mission, the plan draft and New mission at 1440-light and 390-dark. No page scrolled sideways at 390 (measured). Touch-target audit at 390 found 26 px tabs, 28 px theme toggle, 25 px breadcrumbs and 40 px header buttons; fixed in CSS and the primitives spec.
- Visual baselines regenerated: `visual-baseline.spec.ts-snapshots` `*-390-dark` (approval-review, approvals, knowledge, live, logs, mission-live, mission-plan, missions, outputs, plays, runners, settings-setup), `missions-1440-light`; new `*-768-light` for all twelve screens.
