## The company

Keyrus is a data, analytics, AI and digital consultancy. The Middle East and Africa (MEA) business sells services, not goods or civil works.

Domains that fit: data platforms and warehouses (cloud, lakehouse), business intelligence and dashboards (Qlik, Power BI, Tableau), data governance and quality, master data, analytics and AI or machine learning, generative AI, e-government and digital transformation consulting, enterprise performance management and planning, ERP and CRM integration support, cybersecurity advice tied to a data project, training and change management for the same.

Out of scope, mark as skip: construction, supply of hardware only, telecoms networks, medical supplies, security guards, catering, vehicles, printing, translation only.

Rules, mark as skip or maybe with the reason:
- Needs a local company or a local licence only, and no partner is named: maybe, say so.
- Deadline in less than 7 days: maybe, say the deadline is tight.
- Buyer or beneficiary in Syria, or in any country under sanctions: do not recommend. Mark it "compliance check first" and name it.
- Only the notice title is public and the documents cannot be read: say so.

## Priority countries

Tunisia, Morocco, Cote d'Ivoire, Senegal, Algeria, Libya, Mauritania, and the other French-speaking African countries; then Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Jordan, Syria, Iraq and Oman. Look at these first and list them first.

## Sources

Country portals:
- Tunisia, TUNEPS (state e-procurement): https://www.tuneps.tn
- Morocco, public procurement portal: https://www.marchespublics.gov.ma/pmmp/
- Cote d'Ivoire, public procurement regulator (ARCOP) and its notices: https://arcop.ci/
- Senegal, public procurement regulator (ARMP): https://www.armp.sn/
- Mauritania, public procurement regulator (ARMP): https://armp.mr/
- Saudi Arabia, Etimad government tenders: https://tenders.etimad.sa/
- United Arab Emirates, Dubai government e-supply: https://esupply.dubai.gov.ae/
- Bahrain, Tender Board: https://www.tenderboard.gov.bh/
- Oman, e-tendering of the Tender Board: https://etendering.tenderboard.gov.om
- Jordan, JONEPS: https://www.joneps.gov.jo/

Algeria, Libya, Syria and Iraq have no portal that answers from here. Cover them through the multilateral sources below, filtering on the country.

Multilateral and donor sources (cover all priority countries, and Africa and the Middle East in general):
- United Nations Global Marketplace, notices: https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice
- UNDP procurement notices: https://procurement-notices.undp.org
- World Bank procurement notices: https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement
- European Commission funding and tenders portal: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home

Aggregator, for a first look only; never quote it as the buyer's own notice:
- Global Tenders: https://www.globaltenders.com

## Not readable from here

African Development Bank, DgMarket and the Algerian portals refuse automated reading. If a person wants them watched, they go on a list for a person to check.
